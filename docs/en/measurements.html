SLEECH / Measurement coordination

Compare measurements

Normalised deviation En using agreed expanded uncertainties. Default values are illustrative.

Comparison prerequisites

This implementation requires equal coverage factors. Correlated estimates or unequal k need a metrologist calculation. Checkboxes record your statement, not external verification.

Compatibility check

—

En = (x − xref) / √(U² + Uref²)

For |En| ≤ 1, results are compatible under the stated conditions. For |En| > 1, review is needed. Compatibility does not determine product acceptance.

A metrologist signature and a conformity decision rule are separate requirements.

After a disagreement is found

  1. Keep the original series and method.
  2. Check sections, conditions, instruments and uncertainty.
  3. Agree on a testable correction with a metrologist.
  4. Repeat the measurements with the new version and record the conclusion limits.
