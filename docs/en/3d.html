All parts, materials, limits and results are fictional teaching examples. Colour indicates displacement from nominal geometry, not conformity. Limits are illustrative. A real part requires a drawing, method, measurement conditions, uncertainty and a decision rule.

Why it matters

Two inspections of one part may give different values when points, sections or directions differ. Explore how a local high spot, depression or axis offset affects geometry, then relate the shape to the numbers.
