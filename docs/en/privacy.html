Know what happens to your data.

How data is handled

You can browse without registering. The site has no advertising pixels, visitor analytics or third-party font services. Fonts and scripts are served with the site.

CSV files, imported cases, form fields and calculation results are processed in your browser. This version does not upload them to a server or submit requests to the team. Export creates a file on your device. The project team cannot retrieve your drafts through this site.

Avoid entering personal data, trade secrets or restricted information unless needed for your local calculation. You control downloaded files and any material you share with a specialist through another channel.

Device storage

  • Before persistent saving is enabled, drafts and tool state use sessionStorage in the current tab session, including when switching between RU and EN. They normally disappear after the tab closes; browsers may restore sessions.
  • When you choose “Remember drafts”, the case brief is saved automatically and workspace cases are saved with the Save button. This uses localStorage and remains until you delete it or the browser clears the site data.
  • The mode preference is stored in localStorage for up to 180 days to avoid showing the notice on every visit. After that, the site asks again. If storage is unavailable, tools remain usable without guaranteed restoration.
  • Switching to session mode copies the latest saved draft into the tab session. New changes stay in the session; the persistent copy is not erased. Use the button below or browser settings to remove them. Exported files must be deleted separately.

Website hosting

When hosted on GitHub Pages, your browser requests pages from GitHub infrastructure. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP address and device data, under its own policy. This exchange serves the pages and is not controlled by your local-draft preference. GitHub Privacy Statement ↗

Links to standards and other external resources open sites with their own policies. Local case data is not added to these links.

Your choices

Change the mode through Data settings at the bottom of every page. You can also delete saved cases in the workspace and clear the brief in case preparation. Clearing site data in your browser removes saved values and the mode preference.

Project status

Sleech (СЛИЧ) is a project of the Coauthors team. Members and roles are listed on the About page. This is a demonstration prototype. The site does not claim a legal entity, a service contract or an operational data-transfer channel. Before actual service delivery, the parties must agree on the provider, responsibilities and data handling.
