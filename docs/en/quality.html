FROM REQUIREMENT TO CHECK

How we will validate the procedure.

The test plan links the method, raw measurements, software calculation and a specialist’s conclusion.

  1. Agree one measurand

    Record the object, characteristic, section, direction, unit and method version. Results from different sections are not automatically pooled into one repeat series.

  2. Check the specimen and conditions

    Set a stability criterion before the series and check the specimen before and after transport. Preserve equipment and condition records.

  3. Separate statistics and uncertainty

    Mean and spread describe the imported series; they do not replace a justified U. A metrologist checks coverage factor k, correlations and comparison applicability.

  4. Preserve both rounds

    After a method correction, preserve the original series, reason, new version and repeated physical measurements. Report the actual result, including a lack of improvement.

This is a metrological validation plan. The laboratory-stage target is 108 raw results, with validated reference measurements recorded separately. Batch conformity requires a tolerance and an agreed decision rule.
