STAGES AND EVIDENCE

Roadmap.

Every stage has a result and a transition criterion. Dates after the current stage are targets conditional on tests, site access and agreement among participants.

    Available in the demo

    The first use case is defined. The browser demo offers a case passport, CSV validation, result comparison, a 3D lesson and data-report export.

    Evidence for this status

    These are demonstrable software components. The overall procedure has a preliminary self-assessment of TRL 2; industrial readiness still needs testing.

    Open the demo
  2. 022026-11-12
    Planned

    Demand and critical checks

    Investigate recurring customer problems, agree the scope of the first task and verify the calculation independently.

    What confirms this stage

    Target: 12 documented problem interviews, a specification, valid and invalid control inputs, a small physical trial and a metrologist’s review.

  3. 032026-12-24
    Planned

    Laboratory procedure validation

    Two rounds using a stable specimen across three sites. Preserve both series, the method version and the reason for any correction.

    What confirms this stage

    Target: 3 sites × 2 operators × 3 repeats × 3 sections × 2 rounds = 108 results. Reference measurements are separate. Evidence: raw records, stability checks and an expert report.

  4. 042027-02-28
    Planned

    Roles, versions and data exchange

    Test a server prototype with anonymised company formats: independent submissions, locked series and data recovery.

    What confirms this stage

    Evidence: tests of access control, import, report-to-version traceability and backup recovery. Critical calculation and access defects must be resolved.

  5. 052027-04-30
    Planned

    An end-to-end production case

    Demonstrate the prototype for one geometric characteristic with representatives of both parties and a metrologist.

    What confirms this stage

    Evidence: a test programme, a report for one anonymised production case, traceable conclusions and acceptance. TRL 6 is a target conditional on meeting the criteria.

  6. 062027-06-30
    Planned

    Validate the paid offer

    After technical validation, aim for two contracted pilots with an agreed scope, responsibilities and laboratory participation.

    What confirms this stage

    Evidence: two completed paid cases, feedback and actual costs. Payment alone does not establish technical readiness.

  7. 072027-08-31
    Planned

    Accept version 1.0

    Define the supported scope, instructions, support and change-release procedure.

    What confirms this stage

    Evidence: acceptance tests, documentation and verified recovery. Acceptance applies to the agreed scope of tasks.

  8. 082027-12-31
    Planned

    Repeat use

    Assess the stability of the procedure and its economics across consecutive working cases, including repeat use.

    What confirms this stage

    Project target: at least three completed cases over no less than three months, operating records and actual economics.

A status changes when evidence is available. Reaching a calendar date does not by itself complete a stage.

SERVICE LOGIC

Business model canvas.

Working hypothesis: customers pay for an agreed scope of support for a specific task. Demand, service scope and costs still need validation through interviews and pilots.

01

Customer segments

Initial segment: machining and repair businesses in Primorye. Users: metrologists, inspectors and technologists; the buyer is a quality or company manager.

02

Value proposition

A shared method and traceable raw-data package for resolving a particular delivery case. Any time or cost savings must be measured in a pilot.

03

Channels

Plan problem interviews, focused company meetings and a one-scenario demonstration. Validate the need before proposing a limited pilot.

04

Customer relationships

Start with one task. Discuss recurring support when the need repeats. Agree scope and data-exchange rules in advance.

05

Revenue model

One-off work and recurring support have different scopes. Preparation is included in the project; measurement cycles are separate from support. An invited party is not charged a second subscription for the same case.

06

Key activities

Agree the protocol, prepare and validate data, arrange expert review and link a method correction to a new physical measurement series.

07

Key resources

Software prototype, data structure, procedure templates, a stable specimen and suitable measuring equipment. Site access must be confirmed before testing.

08

Key partners

Supplier, customer, a metrologist and an agreed laboratory. External participants are a condition for future cases; active contracts are not claimed.

09

Cost drivers

Expert work, measurements, specimen preparation and logistics, development and support. Validate the economics against the full cost of one agreed case.

DEVELOPMENT CONDITIONS

Progress follows evidence.

If a critical condition is unmet, revise the plan and record the reason. Scale after validating the procedure and its economics.

The specimen changes between sites

Stop the comparison, check stability and select a suitable specimen. A change in the part must not be treated as a measurement error.

The method or data are incompatible

Clarify the measurand, points, units, U and k with a metrologist. Do not use an automated conclusion before checking applicability.

Sites or laboratory resources are unavailable

Perform limited testing and reschedule the full pilot. A one-site result is not presented as three-site validation.

Demand or economics are unproven

Refine the segment and service scope, then check costs. Expand only after confirming demand and repeatability of the procedure.
