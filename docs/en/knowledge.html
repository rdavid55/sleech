Knowledge base

Short explanations linked to working tools. Use them to prepare questions for a metrologist.

What is being measured

Define the measurand, unit, section and direction. The width of an oval surface depends on the angle. Measurements at different points can refer to different quantities. In the 3D lesson, the measurement direction is separate from the camera.

Open the tool
Uncertainty and tolerance

Uncertainty characterises a measurement result. A tolerance is a requirement for the part. An opinion needs the value, unit, U, coverage factor k and a separate conformity decision rule.

Open the tool
Why spread is not U

A mean and range describe repeated observations. A full uncertainty budget may include calibration, resolution, method, temperature and correlations. SLEECH does not turn a CSV range into a complete U.

Open the tool
When En applies

The prototype requires a shared measurand and method, a stable item, positive expanded uncertainties, equal k and independent estimates. If independence is unconfirmed, a metrologist accounts for correlations. En alone does not approve a production lot.

Open the tool
Item stability

Agree on stability checks before and after transport. Damage, heat and surface changes can make results incomparable. Teaching 3D geometry does not establish the stability of a real part.

Quality and conditions
Who is responsible for the opinion

The coordinator agrees on scope; a metrologist reviews the method and calculation; a laboratory is responsible for its measurements; the enterprise approves acceptance. Laboratory accreditation does not automatically cover the platform.

Quality and conditions
How to prepare data

Keep raw values, the operator, instrument, dates, conditions and method version. Check units, missing fields and duplicate observations. Replace the sample CSV with your own data; prototype exports remain unsigned.

Open the tool
What is included in the price

A cycle covers one object, one characteristic, three sections and at most three sites. Preparation is included in a one-off project; paid cycles are excluded from support. The knowledge base and 3D lesson carry no separate fee.
